e2: Ruling R95 (Needs the maintainer): the adopt rule. A higher term moves a node only on the word of a member that may be primary in that node's own view (Can Be Master, or the home it follows), naming itself or any Active member; a Worker never moves a node's term, naming itself or another (R93's guarantee, now about the sender as well as the named node). Why a vouch adds no power: a member that may be primary could promote itself and announce its own term, so letting it name another Active member gives it the same effect it already has; why it is needed: a node that missed a grant (R93's lag) otherwise refuses the real new primary and its own old home forever. Accepted limit: with no trusted member reachable (the old home gone for good) the stranded node stays as it is until it is removed and joined again. Residuals, same class as R94 and closed only by signed terms: a promotable can announce its own term without promote's probe, confirm and restore, can vouch for any Active member including a Worker (or this node), and terms are still unsigned. e2: Ruling R96: `MAX_TERM_STEP` = 64 (a jump larger than 64 terms above the receiver's is refused whatever the sender, checked arithmetic) and `MAX_TERM_N` = i64::MAX (also enforced by the state layer). e2: Ruling R111: node ids stay 64 bits (the first 16 hex characters of the certificate's SHA-256).
