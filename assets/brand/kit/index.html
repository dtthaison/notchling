Notchling brand kit

This is one system: the session-pill N, the three tool dots, and a faceless bot built only from those pieces. Every color comes from the three dots (Claude orange, Codex green, Antigravity blue), plus ink and white. The icon, the menu bar glyph, the phone icon, the shapes, the emotions and the actions are all drawn by the same code (brand.js), so they always match.

4 · N becomes a bot (13 shapes)

Each shape loops: N, then transform, then live, then back to N. The strip shows N, halfway and the shape. Each one has a job in the app, so the bot changes shape to show what Notchling is doing.

5 · Emotions

Each emotion has two clear signals. The pose is exaggerated so the silhouette reads even small. The three dots make a sign anyone knows: a smile arc, a frown arc, a check mark, an ellipsis or an exclamation. There's no face, only pills and dots.

6 · Actions

Notchling's real jobs, acted out by the Visor bot with the same three dots. Props use only ink, white and the three colors.
