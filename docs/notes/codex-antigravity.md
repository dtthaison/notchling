# Codex, Antigravity and the tool contract

The capability contract every tool declares (the generated table is [provider-matrix.md](../provider-matrix.md)), and what bit in Codex rollouts and Antigravity sessions.

Part of the Rust app's notes, moved from `crates/AGENTS.md`; the index is in the root `AGENTS.md`. Keep it true: change the note with the code, and add what bites you under Things that bit before.

- **Contract** (`engine/src/contract/`, decision XXXX). Every tool declares each of 39 capabilities as Native, Fallback, Not applicable or Gap in its own `support.rs` (one exhaustive `match`, never a `_` arm), and `Registry::standard()` answers `support(tool, capability)` and `is_wired`. `gaps.json` and `unwired.json` only shrink (`GUARD-PROVIDER-GAPS`): a PR that wires a cell adds it to its provider's `WIRED` and removes it from `unwired.json`, one that closes a gap removes it from `gaps.json`. `docs/provider-matrix.md` is generated (`NOTCHLING_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1 cargo test -p notchling-engine --test provider_matrix`), `notchling providers [--json]` prints it, and Settings > Agents shows the non-native rows. No surface calls the registry yet; later phases route them. Skill: `add-provider`.

## Things that bit before

- Over a fixture root a Codex turn inherited the daemon's `CODEX_HOME` and wrote to the real `~/.codex`. Children get the folder the daemon reads; a fixture root runs only `<root>/.local/bin/codex`.
- The notch's 2 s poll and Chat's reloads read up to 30 Codex rollouts each time: each one's 256 KB tail (`turn_open`) and, while it had no title, up to 3 MB of its head (`meta`), nearly always unchanged. Both keep their answer until the file's size or time changes (`transcript/codex/stamped.rs`).
- Codex writes each subagent as its own rollout, and a fork of its parent's history sits in it after a second `session_meta`: Chat, the notch and Search listed every such child as a top-level session, and a forked one under the parent's id and first prompt (`codex::meta` kept the last `session_meta`). The first line decides now (`transcript::codex::lineage`: `parent_thread_id`, `thread_source: "subagent"`), `tools::codex::list::session` answers no row for a child, and every Codex reader starts at `transcript::codex::fork::own_start`, the byte where the child's own turn begins, so the parent's `token_count` lines are counted once and its words are not the child's. Search files a child under `SessionMeta.parent` and opens the parent. Fixtures: `crates/engine/tests/fixtures/codex-children/` (made up, one forked with Codex's `subagent_history_start_ordinal`, one forked without it, one not forked).
- A second `agy acp` on a session a delegation holds replays only part, and a reply through it stops the other's work. The daemon finds holders with `lsof` on the brain folder and reads the log instead (`elsewhere::from_lines`: your messages, thinking, tool calls with their results and replies; its item ids come from the line's place in the log, since `step_index` restarts with the session; Chat shows it working from the reply's `working`, and Stop stays off, since `agy.cancel` can't reach another `agy`).
